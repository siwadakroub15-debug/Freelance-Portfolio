SystemSY/01 · Live

AI content calendar pipeline

Gifting Retailer · GCC · Monthly Retainer

Problem:A monthly Instagram calendar meant days of ideation, drafting, and formatting — every single month, from a blank page.

System:An AI content pipeline built on Claude with custom skill files — trained on the brand's identity, buyer personas, funnel stages, and a viral content type framework, wired into Notion as the operating layer.

  • Monthly build: full calendar generated slot by slot with format-locked copy and per-post Notion briefs.
  • Weekly layer: a trend inbox that gets digested, matched to a persona and cycle stage, and built into the live calendar.

Result:A month of on-brand organic content delivered as a working Notion calendar — reviewed and refined instead of written from scratch.

SystemSY/02 · Live

Influencer outreach engine

Ecom Brands · Large Creator Infrastructure

Problem:Influencer marketing at volume meant manually finding creators, vetting profiles, sending DMs one by one, and losing track of who replied.

System:An n8n workflow that scouts influencer leads by scraping Instagram, qualifies them against brand criteria, generates personalized mass outreach, and logs every lead and status change into a Notion tracker.

  • Scraping and lead scoring via Apify feeds a continuously refreshed creator pool.
  • Outreach status lives in one Notion board — scouted, contacted, replied, negotiating, live.

Result:Outreach that used to take a team's week now runs on a schedule — the client's job shrinks to approving creators and closing deals.

SystemSY/03

Reporting & ETL infrastructure

Global Media Network · Adopted Team-Wide

Problem:Weekly performance reporting meant exporting from five different ad managers and manually stitching inconsistent datasets together.

System:A self-built ETL pipeline in Power Query ingesting and standardizing all five sources into one master sheet, plus a dynamic Power BI benchmarking report deriving historical buying rates with objective, platform, and market filtering.

Result:Reporting latency dropped, benchmarks became self-serve for media planning — and the pipeline was adopted by the wider team.
