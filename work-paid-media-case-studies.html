Meta Ads · Tourism · GCC → GlobalCS/01

National Tourism Board — Full-Funnel Destination Marketing

Paid Social Lead · Six-Platform Program

Paid social lead for a national tourism board in the GCC, running Meta as the anchor platform inside a six-platform program (Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, X, Pinterest, LinkedIn). One flagship campaign carried a $3M+ budget across ad platforms, with $760K on Meta alone.

The account ran every major objective in parallel: engagement, video views, traffic, and conversion campaigns optimized to actual ticket sales. Audience strategy was built in layers — retargeting pools from ad engagers and profile engagers, expanded through lookalikes — so each funnel stage fed the next.
